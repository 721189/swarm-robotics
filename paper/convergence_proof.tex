\section{Convergence Analysis}

\subsection{CBBA Convergence Under Ideal Communication}
Choi et al. (2009) proved that under the following assumptions:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item The scoring scheme satisfies the Diminishing Marginal Gains (DMG) property
    \item Communication is complete and instantaneous
\end{enumerate}
CBBA is guaranteed to converge to a conflict-free assignment in a bounded number of iterations.

\subsection{Convergence Under TDMA Constraints}

\textbf{Theorem 1:} Under TDMA communication constraints, the CBBA consensus routine still guarantees convergence to a conflict-free assignment.

\textbf{Proof:}
The consensus routine requires only eventual consistency of the local bid ledgers. TDMA introduces bounded delays but does not prevent eventual consistency.

Let $T_{ideal}$ be the convergence time under ideal communication.
Let $N$ be the number of drones.
Let $\tau$ be the TDMA slot duration.
Let $K$ be the number of consensus rounds required.

Then the convergence time under TDMA is bounded by:
\[
T_{TDMA} \leq T_{ideal} + N \times \tau \times K
\]

\textbf{Discussion:} This bound is linear in the number of drones and slot duration, matching the intuition that larger swarms require more time to reach consensus.
