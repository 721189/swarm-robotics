\subsection{Complexity Analysis}

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\begin{tabular}{|c|c|c|}
\hline
\textbf{Complexity Type} & \textbf{Ideal CBBA} & \textbf{TDMA-CBBA} \\
\hline
Time & $O(n \cdot m \cdot K)$ & $O(n \cdot m \cdot K) + O(n \cdot \tau \cdot K)$ \\
Communication & $O(n \cdot m)$ per round & $O(n \cdot m \cdot N)$ per round \\
Memory & $O(n \cdot m)$ & $O(n \cdot m)$ \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\caption{Complexity comparison between Ideal CBBA and TDMA-CBBA.}
\end{table}

\textbf{Discussion:} The time complexity increases linearly with the slot duration $\tau$ and number of drones $N$, which is consistent with the TDMA scheduling overhead.
